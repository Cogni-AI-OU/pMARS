For those who are interested in running a tournament like this in the
future, try single round tournaments (competitions). It's OK to have
multiple rounds if the rounds involve writing warriors but when you have
obscure rules for different rounds, the individual rounds have no
correlation and hence the tournament becomes a little bit complicated to
judge.

was so different from normal Hill competition.
