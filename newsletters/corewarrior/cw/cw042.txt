Greetings.

The pizza hills are back and so are we.  Sorry for the short vacation, but
there just wasn't much to report on in the last few weeks.  The new 'test'
option is seeing heavy use.  There are disagreements whether tests results
should be included in the standard output reporting option.  Personally, I
would like the option of not receiving test results.  Also, the 'testing'
will greatly reduce artificial hill aging (wasn't that the point?), and along
with it--entries into the Hall of Fame.  Is there something we should do
about this?  Perhaps the most important question about the 'test' option is
whether it is safe or not.  Should test warriors be allowed to ;kill a warrior
on the hill?

--M R Bremer
