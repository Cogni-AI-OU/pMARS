The Hint
Imp/Stone Killing
by Anton Marsden

At present, qscan -> imp/stone is a lethal combination on the Hill. In fact,
roughly half of the top ten warriors are imp/stones. Here are some hints on
how to win more against them.

QScan:

- Boot away fast, using one process if necessary. Try not to give the qscan
a chance to attack you.
- If you have a qscan, scan a lot of locations (more than an imp/stone could
scan). Imps are generally slow to boot, especially if a qscan is done before
booting... this is the best time to neutralise the big imps.

Stone:

- Use spl #1,1 as a decoy where possible (or dat 1,1 if your warrior is a
paper). This way, any decrements the stone attempts on your decoy will slow
it down.

- Use 1,1's whenever possible in bombs. The more 1,1's there are around the
core the better your chance of slowing down the stone.

Imps:

- In general, you don't want to target specific warriors in your coreclear
(if you have one). dclear is probably the best weapon against imps you are
likely to find. However, it can be altered to produce more wins against
3-point imps than a simple dclear:
  - use more than one MOV in the clear - any imps that don't reach the gate
  will have a greater chance of being killed.
  - as a coreclear bomb, try DAT <2667,n. This will weaken 3-point imps
  substantially.
- Some papers use DAT <2667,<2*2667 as an anti-imp bomb.
- If you want to kill 7-point imps, you could use a spiral clear... but this
will make your warrior much less equipped to deal with papers. This clear is
best combined with a scanner. Memories uses a spiral clear quite successfully.


;redcode-94
;name The Machine
;author Anton Marsden
;strategy Blur 2 with some anti-stone improvements
;assert CORESIZE==8000

step EQU 70; mod-10
_gate EQU top
away EQU (clr+1995+660) ; not really :-)

      mov.i  bomb-top,>-1
FOR 3
      dat 0,0
ROF
ptr:  mov.i  inc+1,>step
top:  mov.i  bomb,>ptr
scan: seq.i  2*step,2*step+5
      mov.ab scan,@top
a:    sub.f  inc,scan
      jmn.b  top,@top
inc:  spl.i  #-step,>-step
      mov.i  clr,>_gate
btm:  djn.f  -1,>_gate
clr:  dat.f  <2667,clr-_gate+2
      dat 0,0
      spl.i  #-step,>-step
      dat 0,0
bomb: spl.i  #1,#1
      dat 0,0
      dat 0,0
      mov.i  {-3,<6
      dat 0,0
boot: mov.i  clr,<dest
FOR 8
      mov.i  {boot,<dest
ROF
dest: mov.i  bomb,*away
      spl @dest,{-1000
      mov.i  {boot,<dest
      dat <dest,<dest
      mov.i  {-8,<1
FOR 12
      spl.a  #1,*1
      spl.b  #1,1
      spl.ab #1,@1
      spl.ba #1,}1
      spl.i  #1,#1
ROF
FOR 4
      spl.i  #1,#1
ROF

END boot
