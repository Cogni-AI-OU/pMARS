Extra Extra - Provenance by John Metcalf

Provenance is a oneshot which attempts to take advantage of the excess of
other oneshots on the tiny hill.  During the scan phase, it watches for an
enemy clear approaching.  Should one be detected, it quickly boots away a
d-clear to a safe location.

To determine how effective the technique is, I created a standard oneshot
from Provenance by simply removing the code for the boot / d-clear.  I then
tested both against a selection of tiny oneshots to compare the scores.

The table below shows the results - an overall improvement of 19.1% to the
score.  However the method isn't infallible, and the score is worse against
two of the opponents.

All constants were chosen without computer assistance ;-)  Without further
delay, here's the code:

;redcode-tiny
;name Provenance
;author John Metcalf
;strategy oneshot with escape route
;assert CORESIZE==800

     step  equ 48
     diff  equ 8
     first equ wipe+201
     gate  equ dbmb-1
     safe  equ wipe-40

; ** typical spl/dat clear **

ptr  dat   first,          first-diff

     dat   2,              9
sbmb spl   #-30,           14
wipe mov   @bptr,          >ptr
     mov   @bptr,          >ptr
bptr djn.f wipe,           {sbmb

     for   3
     dat   0,              0
     rof

; ** oneshot / dodger loop **

scan sub   inc,            ptr
     seq   *ptr,           @ptr
     djn.f sbmb,           ptr
     jmz.f scan,           bptr+1  ; check

; ** panic, launch d-clear **

copy mov   @boot,          {boot
     djn   copy,           boot
boot djn.f *safe,          #dclr+2

dbmb dat   <1,             11
inc  spl   #-step,         }-step
dclr mov   dbmb,           >gate
     djn.f dclr,           >gate

     end   scan+1

Extra Extra - Unknown by John Metcalf

After sending one of my tiny warriors to the hill, I issued a challenge to
figure out its strategy from how the warrior scores.  In all, 9 players tried
to determine the solution, but only German Labarga and Christian Schmidt came
up with the right answer.

Unknown is a clear/imp.  It uses a d-clear with an a-field gate and a vector
launched a-imp spiral.  Two values have been computer optimised, iboot and
the initial contents of gate.

After arriving on the hill in 3rd place, it dropped quickly, leaving age 21.
Unknown hopefully shows clear/imp can be a successful strategy on the tiny
hill and maybe a few more redcoders will give them a try.

So finally, here's the code you've been waiting for:

;redcode-tiny
;name Unknown
;author John Metcalf
;strategy puzzle in miniature
;strategy to win a copy of my code,
;strategy can you tell me what I am?
;assert CORESIZE==800

        iboot equ imp+69
        iStep equ 267

gate    dat   iboot+80,      0

dbmb    dat   8,             1

        dat   0,             0

clear   spl   #0,            0
cloop   mov   dbmb,          }gate
        djn.f cloop,         }gate

        for   3
        dat   0,             0
        rof

imp     mov.i #iStep,        *0

vect    spl   iboot+iStep*1, iboot
        spl   iboot+iStep*3, iboot+iStep*2
        jmp   iboot+iStep*5, iboot+iStep*4

less    spl   clear,         }iboot+iStep*6
        mov   imp,           iboot
        spl   1,             }-261
        spl   1,             {-283
        spl   2,             {-305
        jmp   @vect,         }0
        jmp   *vect,         }0
        end   less
