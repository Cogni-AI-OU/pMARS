and techniques for the tiny hills.  If you haven't yet discovered the
tiny hills, the specs are:

         coresize: 800
   max. processes: 800

 minimum distance: 20

code for Tiny Tiny Bombers and Lapidem Mortiferum, two unusual strategies

Tiny Tiny Bombers by John Metcalf

TTB launches lots of small bombers like Hydra or Return of the Living Dead
and is based on this CWS'88 example published by David Moore in 2006:

        spl    1
        spl    1
        mov    -1
        spl    0,     <2
        mov    0,     <1
        mov    4,     978
        add    #2201, @-2
        mov    -4,    @-2
        jmp    @-3,   0
        djn    -2,    #6970

David's example continuously launches small anti-imp clears of the form:

        spl    0,     <2
        mov    0,     <1          ; anti-imp bomb
        djn    -2,    #6970

TTB is a straight conversion of David's code to CWS'94 and launches 12
clears before self-bombing.  MAXPROCESSES is reached after 1929 cycles,
faster than a typical tiny silk.

It would be interesting to see how well TTB pairs with paper or imps.
Here's the current version with a tiny qscan which entered the tiny hill
in 15th place:

;redcode-tiny
;name Tiny Tiny Bombers
;author John Metcalf
;strategy launch lots of tiny tiny bombers
;assert CORESIZE==800

        qx     equ 504
        qy     equ 701

        sne.x  qf+((qx-1)*qy+1)*(((qx-1)*qy)%800), qf+(qx-1)*qy
        seq.x  qf+(qx*qy+1)*((qx*qy)%800),         }qf
        jmp    @qlo+1,                             {qf
        sne.x  qf+((qx+1)*qy+1)*(((qx+1)*qy)%800), qf+(qx+1)*qy
        jmz.f  tspl,                               <qf

qf      mul.x  #qx,        #qy    ; decode
        jmz.f  @qlo+1,     >qf

qlo     mov    }519,       >qf    ; attack
        mov    }qlo,       {qf
        seq    {qf,        >qf
        djn.f  qlo,        >qf

        first  equ 88
        step   equ -351

tspl    spl    #352,       <boot
        mov.i  #1,         <boot
boot    mov    tdjn,       first
        sub    #-step,     boot
        mov    tspl,       @boot
        djn.f  @boot,      {tspl

tdjn    djn    -2,         #147

Lapidem Mortiferum by Christian Schmidt

Lapidem Mortiferum is an unusual self-splitting stone/clear with each new
process executing the stone exactly once before dropping through into the
SPL/DAT clear.

As processes build up in the clear it rapidly takes over from the stone.
The stone executes 37% of the time during the first 100 cycles, dropping
to 19% in the next 100 and only 6% in the 100 after that.

The same technique could be used to pair other strategies although it
remains to be seen whether or not they could repeat the success of
Lapidem Mortiferum.

Here's the code for Lapidem Mortiferum with a 3c decoy maker which
entered the tiny hill in 8th place:

;redcode-tiny
;name Lapidem Mortiferum
;author Christian Schmidt
;assert 1
;strategy dwarf with a sneaky trick

sStep1  equ     340
sStep2  equ     323
sStep3  equ     715
sStep4  equ     739
sGate   equ     sGo-3
sGap    equ     sGo+16
sTrail  equ     702

sDec1   equ     45
sDec2   equ     sGo+104

sGo     spl     #sStep1 , >sStep2
sAtt    mov     {sStep3 , {sStep4
        add.f   sGo     , sAtt
        mov.i   @sLoop  , >sGate
sLoop   djn.f   -1      , {bomb
        dat     }sTrail , }sGap
bomb    spl     #sTrail , }sGap

        for    6
        dat    0,0
        rof

;-------Decoymaker-------------------
sCnt
z       for    6
        mov    <sDec2+sDec1*z, {sDec2+sDec1*z+sDec1-1
        rof
        djn.f  sGo,      <sDec2+sDec1*8
;-------Decoymaker-------------------

end     sCnt
