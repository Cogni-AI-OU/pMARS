LP Paper
by Fizmo
Part I: The idea behind singel process replication

This time I would like to introduce you to LP Papers. They're working different
to the common silk-style papers you may know from the other hills. On the
LP hill you just have 8 processes available to replicate. To generate the
maximum possible number of active replicates one can give each copy only a
single process. But each copy should be able to generate more than one copy
if needed. So, how to achieve all this goals in the most efficient way?

Let's have a look to the following five line of code:


from    mov.ab  #5,     #0
loop    mov.i   <from,  {to
        jmn.b   loop,   from
again   spl.b   >from,  0
to      jmz.b   AWAY,   *0


The first instruction just sets a counter in its b-field. That is the number of
instruction the LP paper will copy. The second and third instruction copies the
code starting from the 'to'. If the last instruction is copied the b-field of
'from' is zero, so that the process falling through the jmn instruction. If the
max. processes aren't reach the fourth instruction starts the replication with
'from'. With the last instruction 'to' the LP paper jumps to the copied code.
The jmz is used to check if the b-field of the copied 'from' instruction is
still zero. That means the copied code seems still intact.

Extra Extra - Vr'ugugat by Christian Schmidt

Talking about LP paper. Here's a new one I wrote. It's based on the lp paper
'Paper With Eyes' which scans and replicates to the location where it founds
something. With Vr'ugugat I did it vice versa. It replicates before scanning.
As scanner I used a simple add/jmz scanning engine with a variable dat-wipe.
Even I hit my own copies it shouldn't hurt the replication. I choosed the
'dat >5334, >2667' bomb for some extra points against imp-warriors. On the
DynaHill it hopefully get some wins agains paper/imps and stone/imps as well.
Although it will loose against most scanner types.


;redcode-lp
;name Vr'ugugat
;author Christian Schmidt
;assert 1
;strategy lp replication, scan, dat-wipe
;strategy totally unoptimized

ConA EQU  7627
ConB EQU  2786
ConC EQU  2646
ConD EQU  13

from   mov     #ConD,      #ConD
loop   mov     <from,   {to
       jmn     loop,    from
to     spl     ConA,    ConB
       add.a   #ConC,   to
       spl     from,   0
scan   add.b   to,     #0
       jmz.f   scan,   @scan
       mov.i   2,      >scan
       jmp     -2,     0
       dat     >5334   , >2667

end
