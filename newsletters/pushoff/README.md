# _PUSH OFF_

Paul Kline published the _PUSH OFF_ newsletters between March 1993 and February 1994, and his commentary captured classic moments in Core War history - the death of Sphinx, the rise and fall of vamp/imps, and much more. In all, there were 28 issues. The newsletter is also available online at [corewar.co.uk](https://corewar.co.uk/pushoff.htm).
