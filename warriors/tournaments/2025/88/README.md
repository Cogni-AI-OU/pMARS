# Core War Tournament Weekend 2025 - '88 Tournament

The '88 tournament took place on Saturday 15th November 2025 at the Retro Computer Festival in Cambridge, UK.

Warriors were added to the top-128 of the 88-Koenigstuhl and then competed in a diversity push-off tournament. Full results are available at [corewar.co.uk/tournamentweekend2025.htm](https://corewar.co.uk/tournamentweekend2025.htm).

## Tournament Settings

- **Standard**: CWS'88
- **Command**: `pmars -8 -s 8000 -p 8000 -c 80000 -l 100 -d 100`
- **Coresize**: 8000
- **Processes**: 8000
- **Cycles**: 80000
- **Length**: 100
- **Distance**: 100
