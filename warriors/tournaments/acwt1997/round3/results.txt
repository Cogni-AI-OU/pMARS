The quality of entries in this round was very good.

I've decided to end the tournament here because quite a few players can't
play any more rounds and I'm running out of ideas for rounds :-)

I'll publish the entries for all rounds soon... I'm not going to declare a
winner for the tournament because of the nature of the tournament... you
can calculate your final score if you're interested though.

For those who are interested in running a tournament like this in the
future, try single round tournaments (competitions). It's OK to have
multiple rounds if the rounds involve writing warriors but when you have
obscure rules for different rounds, the individual rounds have no
correlation and hence the tournament becomes a little bit complicated to
judge.

However, I think most people found the tournament interesting because it
was so different from normal Hill competition.
