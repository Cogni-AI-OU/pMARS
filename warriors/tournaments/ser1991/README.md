## 1er TORNEO de REDCODE (Sociedad Española de Redcode)

The first Spanish Core War tournament was held in Granada in late 1991, organized by J. J. Merelo of the University of Granada.

### Results

1. **Arania** by Cristobal Jiménez López & Juan A. Molina Corpas
2. **R.I.P.** by Jose María Badía García
3. **KK** by Juan Manuel Rodríguez Ibañez

The winners advanced to the [1991 ICWS Tournament](../icwt1991).
