# IRC Mini Tournament #02 - Sunday 09 March 2003

This week, Phil Thorne organises the Mini Tournament, with just 30 minutes available to write a tiny hill warrior. The compiled Redcode is not allowed to contain the numbers -1 or 1. Warriors battle round robin for 250 rounds. All in, 6 players enter a total of 10 warriors and Roy van Rijn emerges the clear winner, taking the top two positions.

## Details

- **Coresize**: 800
- **Cycles**: 8000
- **Max Processes**: 800
- **Max Length**: 20
- **Max Distance**: 20
- **Restriction**: The load file must not contain the number 1 or -1.
- **Rounds**: 250
- **Command Line**: `pmars -s 800 -c 8000 -p 800 -l 20 -d 20 -F NNN`
