# IRC Mini Tournament #11 - Sunday 11 May 2003

Lukasz Grabun hosts the 11th contest within an 8192 cell core, under restricted '88 rules. The instructions available are limited to just six, **mov**, **add**, **jmp**, **spl**, **cmp** and **dat**. Nine authors enter thirteen warriors in total, with John Metcalf taking the first two places with an imp spiral and a oneshot.
