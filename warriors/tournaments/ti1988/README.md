## Tournament: Primo Torneo Italiano di Core Wars (Oct 1988)

This tournament was held as part of the Core War community competitions in Italy.
Lux 2 by Luca Crosara won 1st place.

### Warriors
- [Lux 2](lux2.red) by Luca Crosara (1st Place)
- [Sturm und Drang](sturmunddrang.red) by G. Ciraolo & L. Pancani (2nd Place)
- [Panca 1](panca1.red) by L. Pancani & G. Ciraolo (3rd Place)

Source: [Core War: Tournaments](https://corewar.co.uk/torneoitaliano1988.htm)
