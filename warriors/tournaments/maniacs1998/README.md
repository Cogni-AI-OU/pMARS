# Redcode Maniacs Tournament (1998)

The Redcode Maniacs Tournament was organized by Christian Schmidt and Philip Kendall between January and April 1998. It consisted of 9 rounds with varying rules and constraints.

## Tournament Rounds

- **[Round 1](./round1/README.md)**: Standard 94 Draft, length 50.
- **[Round 2](./round2/README.md)**: Low Process (LP) challenge, max processes 8, length 200.

## Overall Results

- **Chief Maniac**: David Moore
- **Deputy Chief Maniac**: Robert Macrae
