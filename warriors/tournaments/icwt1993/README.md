## Tournament: 1993 ICWS Tournament (Jan 1994)

The eighth Annual ICWS Tournament was held in January 1994. The first prize of $35 was claimed by Paul Kline and the $15 second prize by Anders Ivner. Jon Newman posted the results to rec.games.corewar.

### Configuration
- **Standard:** CWS'88
- **Coresize:** 8192
- **Processes:** 8000
- **Cycles:** 100000
- **Rounds:** 100

### Top Warriors
1. **Cannonade** by Paul Kline
2. **Leprechaun 1b** by Anders Ivner
3. **Agony T** by Stefan Strack
4. **Smartbomb 4.0** by Devin Kilminster
5. **Irony v1.0** by Brant D. Thomsen

Source: [Core War: The 1993 ICWS Tournament](https://corewar.co.uk/icwt1993.htm)
