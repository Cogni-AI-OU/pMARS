# 1991 ICWS Tournament Results

The 6th Annual ICWS Tournament was held in early 1992. Sixteen warriors fought in a round-robin tournament with self-fights and two rounds per match. The tournament was governed by CWS'88.

- **Scoring:** 3 points per win, 1 point per tie
- **Format:** Round-robin with self-fights, 2 rounds per match
