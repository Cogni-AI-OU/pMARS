## Tournament: 1991 ICWS Tournament (1992)

The 6th Annual ICWS Tournament was held in early 1992, with entries submitted by the Spanish Branch Section (Sociedad Española de Redcode) and the Electronic Branch Section.

Sixteen warriors fought in a round-robin tournament, with self-fights, and two rounds per match. The tournament was governed by CWS'88, 3 points per win, 1 point for a tie.

### Top Finishers

1. **[XTC](xtc.red)** by Stefan Röttger
2. **Fernandez**
3. **[Vlad](vlad.red)** by David Bofinger & T. J. Allen
4. **Arania** by Cristobal Jiménez López & Juan A. Molina Corpas
5. **[Quarter](quarter.red)** by Stefan Hänßgen
