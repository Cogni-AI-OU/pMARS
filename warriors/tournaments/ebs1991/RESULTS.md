## Tournament Rules

- **Standard:** CWS'88
- **Coresize:** 8192
- **Cycles:** 100,000
- **Processes:** 64
- **Scoring:** 3 points per win, 1 point per tie
- **Format:** One-on-one round robin with self-fights
